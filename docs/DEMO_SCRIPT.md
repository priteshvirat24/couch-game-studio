# Demo video: Couch Game Studio (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

**Recording setup**
- Fire TV footage: the Vega Virtual Device on the Apple-silicon Mac, screen-recorded with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording, select the VVD window). The rules require visible Fire TV / Vega simulator footage, so keep the VVD window frame visible at least once.
- Phone footage: record the phone screen (iOS: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record).
- Edit side by side: phone on the left (about 30%), TV on the right (about 70%).
- Real kid voice if possible (with a parent's consent). Otherwise an adult reading the kid's lines works.
- No copyrighted music. The in-game chiptune is generated by the app.

**Before recording**
1. `npm start`, then `cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8787`. Put the tunnel URL in `vega/src/config.ts`, then `vega build` and `vega run-app`.
2. Pre-create 1–2 games so the Family Arcade looks lived-in (the seeds are already there).
3. Have a crayon drawing ready (a simple monster or a pet works best on white paper).
4. Make sure Amazon Bedrock is configured, so games are designed live by the AI (the offline designer is the fallback).

**Editing tips**
- Lead with the TV. Judges should see Fire TV footage within the first 15 seconds.
- Show the phone and TV at the same moment the game appears. That cause and effect is the "wow".
- Keep captions on for every spoken line (accessibility, and many judges watch muted).
- Export at 1080p and upload to YouTube as **Public** (not Unlisted, or check the rules), in English.
